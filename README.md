# Zombie Over 🧟‍♂️

A top-down zombie survival shooter game built with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript.

## About the Game

Survive waves of increasingly difficult zombies! Defend your position, manage your ammo, and rack up as high a score as possible before the horde overwhelms you.

## Features

- **Top-Down Shooter Gameplay**: Navigate and aim with your mouse, shoot with clicks
- **Wave System**: Zombies get faster and stronger with each wave
- **Health & Ammo System**: Manage your limited ammunition and health
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Each wave adds more enemies with increased stats
- **Score Tracking**: Earn more points for each zombie defeated in higher waves
- **Real-time HUD**: Monitor health, ammo, wave, and score

## How to Play

### Controls

- **Movement**: Arrow Keys or WASD
- **Aim**: Move your mouse
- **Shoot**: Left Click
- **Reload**: Press R

### Gameplay Tips

1. Keep moving to avoid enemy clusters
2. Manage your ammo wisely - reload when safe
3. Prioritize zombies getting close to you
4. Each wave gets progressively harder
5. Your score multiplier increases with each wave

## Game Mechanics

- **Player**: 30x30 green square with rotating gun barrel
- **Enemies**: Red squares that spawn from screen edges and move toward you
- **Bullets**: Yellow projectiles that deal 1 damage per hit
- **Health System**: Start with 100 health, lose 1 per zombie contact
- **Wave Progression**: Advance to next wave after defeating all zombies
